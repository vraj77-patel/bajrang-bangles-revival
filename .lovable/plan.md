
# Shree Bajrang Bangles Pipe — Website Plan

## Overview
A professional, attractive single-page scrolling website for **Shree Bajrang Bangles Pipe**, an acrylic bangles pipe manufacturer. The site will feature a **blue & white** color theme with smooth animations and an animated product collage grid.

---

## Sections (Top to Bottom)

### 1. **Header & Navigation**
- Sticky top navbar with the uploaded company logo on the left
- Navigation links: Home, About, Products, Contact
- Clean blue & white styling with smooth scroll to sections

### 2. **Hero Banner**
- Bold headline: "Shree Bajrang Bangles Pipe" with a tagline about quality acrylic bangles pipes
- Eye-catching background with gradient blue tones
- Call-to-action button (e.g., "View Products" / "Contact Us")
- Fade-in entrance animation

### 3. **About Us**
- Company story, mission, and values
- Highlight years of experience, quality commitment, and customer trust
- Animated fade-in on scroll

### 4. **Products — Animated Collage Grid**
- Beautifully animated product grid showcasing acrylic bangles pipes and other acrylic products
- Cards that fade-in and scale-up as user scrolls into view
- Each product card shows an image placeholder, product name, and brief description
- Staggered animation for a dynamic collage effect
- Product categories for different types/sizes

### 5. **Why Choose Us**
- Key differentiators displayed as icon cards (Quality, Variety, Best Price, Timely Delivery)
- Hover animations on each card

### 6. **Contact Us**
- Phone number, WhatsApp link (click to chat), Email, and Physical Address
- A simple contact form (Name, Phone, Message)
- Embedded map placeholder for factory/shop location
- WhatsApp floating button in the corner for quick access

### 7. **Footer**
- Company logo, quick links, contact info summary
- Social media placeholders
- Copyright notice

---

## Design & Animations
- **Color Theme**: Blue (#1E40AF shades) & White, with subtle gold accents
- **Collage Animation**: Product grid items animate in with staggered fade + scale effects as user scrolls
- **Smooth scroll** navigation between sections
- **Hover effects** on buttons and product cards
- **Responsive design** for mobile, tablet, and desktop

---

## Notes
- User will upload the company logo after implementation begins
- Product images will use attractive placeholders initially — user can replace with real photos later
- No backend needed — this is a static informational website
- Contact form will show a success toast (no email sending without backend)
